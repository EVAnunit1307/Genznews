What does it mean to mourn? A question I ask myself often is: how, every time I see an elderly person, do I let nostalgia consume me, then ask myself, will mourning last a lifetime? When does it end how come I cannot live without the thought of him consuming me the thought of loss? I sometimes wonder what it is that is so endearing to miss the memories, the warmth, the unconditional love I was once embraced with. Is mourning an ever-ending process of suffering although there is an underlying beauty to mourning to remember someone every day, to think of who he once was, does that bring him comfort in the afterlife to know someone remembers him? The afterlife within itself is a complex process to explain what it means to be dead, religions across the world come to challenge this question but none can be true can they hence if he is gone is he truly gone isn't his body decaying to a corpse the fragility of human life makes me wonder why one makes and procreates. Mourn: I am a mourner and it has no meaning itself to mourn is not to suffer but it’s also not to find joy; it's to remember let endless nostalgia consume myself into nothing but memories that I cannot escape— mourning doesn’t make or change the dead it gives the living comfort.
